erikterm icon variants

Variations on the two concepts you liked, A (prompt tile) and B (lowercase e), plus three hybrids. Everything uses the Catppuccin Macchiato palette (lavender #b7bdf8, blue #8aadf4, mauve #c6a0f6 on #363a4f→#1e2030) and no glows. Each card shows the icon large, at 64/32/24/16 px on dark and light, the actual 16/24/32 px rasters zoomed, and a launcher mock with generic neighbours.

Small sizes here are plain downscales of the 64-unit master. Once you pick one, it gets hand-tuned 16/24/32 px drawings snapped to the pixel grid.

References

The two concepts as you saw them.

A

Prompt tile (original)

Reference: rounded chevron and a blue block cursor on the deep tile.

B

Lowercase e (original)

Reference: a geometric e with an underscore cursor at its foot.

A · Prompt tile

Chevron and cursor on the deep tile.

A1

Underline cursor

Chevron plus an underscore cursor (❯_), the classic shell look.

A2

Slim caret

A thin text-caret bar instead of the block: lighter, more editor-like.

A3

Gradient ink

Chevron and cursor share one top-to-bottom lavender to blue gradient.

A4

Cooler tile, top bevel

Bluer tile with a hairline highlight on the inner top edge (a bevel, not a glow).

A5

Warmer tile

Mauve-tinted tile and rim; same lavender chevron and blue cursor.

A6

Squircle tile

Superellipse tile (continuous corners, like GNOME/Fluent app icons).

B · Lowercase e

The e as the mark, with the cursor as its accent.

B1

Cursor in the opening

The bowl stops early and a block cursor fills the e's mouth.

B2

Prompt crossbar

The crossbar starts as a prompt arm (❯) and runs into the bowl.

B3

Heavy stroke

Bolder e (stroke 8) that holds up better at 16 px.

B4

Light stroke, lavender to mauve

Finer e (stroke 4.5) in a lavender to mauve gradient.

AB · Hybrids

Where the e meets the prompt.

AB1

e as the prompt

An e takes the chevron's place, followed by the block cursor (can read as "el").

AB2

Cursor crossbar

The e's crossbar is a blue cursor bar; the bowl stays lavender.

AB3

Terminal-font e

A squared monospace e (straight stems, crisp on the pixel grid) with an underscore cursor.

All variants in a row
